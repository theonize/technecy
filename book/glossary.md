# Glossary

Working vocabulary from all three disciplines. Each entry notes the chapter where it does its main work.

## A–C

- **Apodictic / casuistic law** — Unconditional commands ("thou shalt not...") vs. conditional case law ("if a man... then..."): invariants vs. case logic. [Ch. 7](07-law-as-code-covenant-as-protocol.md)
- **Apokalypsis** (Gk.) — "Unveiling"; disclosure, not catastrophe. The genre-name of Revelation. [Ch. 14](14-apocalypse-the-great-unveiling.md)
- **Binah** (Heb.) — Understanding; from the root "between" — the capacity to discern, to draw the distinction that matters. [Ch. 9](09-from-data-to-wisdom.md)
- **Canon** (Gk. *kanōn*, "measuring rod") — The authenticated collection of Scripture; in this book, also any curated baseline against which new claims are verified. [Ch. 5](05-the-canon-an-error-corrected-channel.md), [15](15-practicing-technecy.md)
- **Channel** — The medium through which a message travels, characterized by capacity and noise. The biblical text's channel: three millennia of hand-copying. [Ch. 5](05-the-canon-an-error-corrected-channel.md)
- **Cheirographon** (Gk.) — A handwritten certificate of debt; Colossians 2:14's word for the record canceled at the cross. [Ch. 13](13-the-book-of-life.md)
- **Checksum** — A computed value used to verify data integrity; the functional description of the Masoretes' letter-counts. [Ch. 5](05-the-canon-an-error-corrected-channel.md)
- **Chiasm** — Mirror-structured text (A-B-C-C′-B′-A′); emphasis lives at the fold. Example: Genesis 9:6. [Ch. 6](06-figures-of-speech-divine-compression.md)
- **Chokhmah** (Heb.) — Wisdom — literally *skill, craft*: Bezalel's metalwork and the sailor's seamanship are both chokhmah. The root of this book's title-concept. [Ch. 9](09-from-data-to-wisdom.md)
- **Compression** — Encoding meaning in fewer symbols; the information role of metaphor and figure. [Ch. 6](06-figures-of-speech-divine-compression.md)
- **Covenant** — A formal relationship-establishing document/act: preamble, history, stipulations, archive, audit, signs. Distinguished from bare law as protocol is from rules. [Ch. 7](07-law-as-code-covenant-as-protocol.md)

## D–I

- **Davar** (Heb.) — Word *and* thing/matter: one noun for speech and reality. [Ch. 1](01-in-the-beginning-was-information.md)
- **DIKW** — The data → information → knowledge → wisdom hierarchy; useful as a map of what can go missing. [Ch. 9](09-from-data-to-wisdom.md)
- **Docetism** — The early heresy that Christ only *seemed* (dokein) embodied; the perennial temptation to treat patterns as more real than flesh. [Ch. 10](10-the-word-made-flesh.md)
- **Duplication problem** — If a person is a pattern, a perfect copy is equally the person; therefore pattern-continuity cannot be the whole of personal identity. [Ch. 12](12-memory-identity-resurrection.md)
- **Entropy (Shannon)** — Average surprise per symbol; measures unpredictability, not meaning — noise scores high. [Ch. 1](01-in-the-beginning-was-information.md)
- **Error correction** — Recovering a message from corrupted copies via redundancy; what textual criticism does with manuscripts. [Ch. 5](05-the-canon-an-error-corrected-channel.md)
- **Figure of speech** — A deliberate departure from plain usage, functioning as emphasis markup; Bullinger catalogued 200+ in Scripture. [Ch. 6](06-figures-of-speech-divine-compression.md)
- **Goodhart's law** — When a measure becomes a target it ceases to measure; the Pharisee failure mode (Matthew 23:23). [Ch. 7](07-law-as-code-covenant-as-protocol.md)
- **Hylomorphism** — Aristotle's (and Aquinas's) view that the soul is the *form of the body* — an embodied unity, not software on hardware. [Ch. 10](10-the-word-made-flesh.md)
- **Icon / index / symbol** — Peirce's three sign-types: by resemblance, by causal connection, by convention. [Ch. 2](02-the-image-and-the-icon.md)
- **Imago Dei** (Lat.) — The image of God: humanity as God's living, authorized representation. [Ch. 2](02-the-image-and-the-icon.md)

## L–P

- **Logos** (Gk.) — Word, reason, ordering principle; John 1's name for the one by whom all things were made — pattern grounded in Person. [Ch. 1](01-in-the-beginning-was-information.md), [10](10-the-word-made-flesh.md)
- **Masorah** — The Masoretes' marginal apparatus of counts and notes: integrity metadata wrapped around the Hebrew text. [Ch. 5](05-the-canon-an-error-corrected-channel.md)
- **Mystērion** (Gk.) — Not a permanent puzzle but a secret kept until its time and then published. [Ch. 14](14-apocalypse-the-great-unveiling.md)
- **Nominalism / realism** — Whether categories are human labels only, or track real structure; Genesis 2:19 splits the difference (stewarded realism). [Ch. 3](03-naming-the-animals.md)
- **Ontology** — Philosophy: the study of what exists. Computing: a formal schema of entities and relations. Genesis 1–2 involves both. [Ch. 3](03-naming-the-animals.md)
- **Parallelism** — Hebrew poetry's semantic rhyme; doubles as redundancy and survives translation — the translation-proof poetic layer. [Ch. 6](06-figures-of-speech-divine-compression.md)
- **Performative** — Austin's term for utterances that *do* rather than describe ("let there be light" as the limiting case). [Ch. 1](01-in-the-beginning-was-information.md)
- **Protocol** — An agreed structure enabling communication/relationship between parties; what Babel broke and covenant formalizes. [Ch. 4](04-babel-when-protocols-break.md), [7](07-law-as-code-covenant-as-protocol.md)

## R–W

- **Redundancy** — Extra encoding that defeats noise: manuscript abundance, parallel passages, four Gospels, memorized liturgy. [Ch. 5](05-the-canon-an-error-corrected-channel.md)
- **Root of trust** — The unverified anchor that grounds a verification chain; Proverbs 1:7 locates it in the fear of the LORD. [Ch. 9](09-from-data-to-wisdom.md)
- **Selah** — Psalm notation, probably "pause"; in this book, the discipline of the gap between message and reaction. [Ch. 15](15-practicing-technecy.md)
- **Shem** (Heb.) — Name, carrying character and role; renamings (Abram→Abraham) as schema migrations. [Ch. 3](03-naming-the-animals.md)
- **Speech act** — An utterance analyzed as an action (Austin, Searle): what it says, what it does, what it effects. [Ch. 1](01-in-the-beginning-was-information.md)
- **Suzerainty treaty** — The ancient Near Eastern vassal-treaty format mirrored by Exodus/Deuteronomy: preamble, prologue, stipulations, deposit, witnesses, sanctions. [Ch. 7](07-law-as-code-covenant-as-protocol.md)
- **Technē** (Gk.) — Craft, skill, art — knowledge that lives in a maker's hands; with *chokhmah*, the root of **technecy**: the craft of the word. [Preface](00-preface.md), [Ch. 9](09-from-data-to-wisdom.md)
- **Textual criticism** — The discipline of inferring an original text from its surviving copies: error correction as scholarship. [Ch. 5](05-the-canon-an-error-corrected-channel.md)
- **Type / token** — The abstract pattern vs. its concrete instance; incarnation as the scandal of instantiation. [Ch. 10](10-the-word-made-flesh.md)
- **Weaver's three levels** — A: technical accuracy; B: semantic meaning; C: effectiveness. Engineering solved A; Scripture claims all three. [Ch. 1](01-in-the-beginning-was-information.md)
